Customer enquiries

Booking Management

Review requests, update status, record admin-only advance payments and notes.

Advance payments, remaining balances, payment history and admin notes are private owner records. They must never be shown on the customer booking page.
Booking requests will load here when Firestore is configured.
Rates & packages

Services & Pricing

Manage the service catalogue and rates. Prices are controlled by the studio owner.

Traditional Photo Shoot is complimentary (₹0) as specified. Other prices should be entered only after the studio confirms the actual rates. Event-specific rates can differ.
Service catalogue is readyConnect the secure Firestore rules to save and load service rates.
Photos & films

Media Library

Review studio uploads, control gallery visibility and organise media.

Media upload and Cloudinary asset deletion require the signed-upload Worker to be configured. This page can list existing media records and change publication status once Firestore rules are updated.
Existing studio media records will appear here when connected.

Website Content
