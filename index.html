About the Studio

Our Story

Sanjay Photo Studio & Films is a photography and filmmaking studio focused on preserving the moments that make every occasion special. From weddings and family celebrations to pre-wedding shoots, portraits and events, the studio brings photography and video services together in one place.

Every project begins with understanding the occasion and the customer's expectations. Our aim is to make the process clear and comfortable, capture meaningful moments with care, and deliver photographs and films that customers can revisit for years.

About the Owner

The studio is guided by its owner, who is the main point of contact for customers and oversees each assignment from enquiry to delivery. The owner's name and personal introduction can be added here once the preferred details are provided.
